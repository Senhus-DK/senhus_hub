Privacy Policy for Mary

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Mary is a private household assistant used for personal task management, reminders, scheduling, and related assistant functions.

Information processed

Mary may process information that users choose to send to the service, including:

  • WhatsApp messages sent directly to Mary
  • The sender's phone number and basic message metadata
  • Task and reminder details
  • Dates, times, names, and other information included in messages

How information is used

Information is processed only to provide requested assistant functions, including interpreting requests, creating reminders, managing tasks, scheduling, and sending requested responses or notifications.

Service providers

Messages and task information may be processed by infrastructure, automation, messaging, and AI service providers that are necessary to operate Mary. Information is not sold or rented to advertisers.

Data retention

Information may be retained only as needed to provide the requested assistant functions, maintain the service, or comply with applicable legal requirements. Stored information may be deleted when it is no longer needed.

Data deletion and questions

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Mary, or ask questions about this policy, by contacting:

info@senhus.dk

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Mary's functionality or data handling changes. The latest version will be published at this page.
